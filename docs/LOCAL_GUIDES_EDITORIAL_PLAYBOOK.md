# Central Illinois Local Pros — Local Guides Editorial Playbook

## Purpose

Local Guides are useful editorial content that helps Central Illinois residents and visitors make better local decisions and discover relevant directory businesses. They are not thin SEO filler and are not paid rankings.

## Core guide types

- Community Guide — city, downtown, neighborhood, history, attractions and local-day content.
- Homeowner Guide — practical home-service hiring, maintenance and comparison education.
- Legal Guide — general educational preparation and attorney-comparison content; never personalized legal advice.
- Dining Guide — local dining planning, food traditions and restaurant-comparison education.
- Shopping Guide — independent retail, antiques, markets and specialty-shopping content.
- City & Category Guide — a deeper article tied directly to one city/category directory page.

## Publication standards

Flagship guides should normally contain:

1. A useful, non-clickbait title with the city or Central Illinois intent when relevant.
2. A concise summary that accurately describes the article.
3. Several meaningful H2 sections.
4. Practical steps, checklists or itinerary structure when appropriate.
5. Internal links to relevant city/category directory pages.
6. Official or primary-source links for factual tourism, government, historical or regulated information when useful.
7. A reminder to verify current hours, inventory, event schedules or professional details directly.
8. No unsupported superlatives such as “best,” “#1,” or “top-rated.”
9. No fabricated ratings, testimonials, rankings, lead data or business performance claims.
10. A body substantial enough to answer the search intent rather than repeating the directory page.

## Local-business integrity

A guide may describe a category, district or local experience without ranking directory businesses. Paid sponsorship must never determine organic editorial recommendations or organic directory position.

When named businesses are included in future editorial content, factual claims should be source-backed and current. A business being listed in the directory does not imply verification, endorsement or guaranteed quality.

## SEO and internal linking

Every flagship guide should connect to the most relevant live directory path:

- City guide → `/illinois/{city}`
- City/category guide → `/illinois/{city}/{category}`
- Broad category guide → category search or the applicable vertical page

City and category pages should surface relevant guides automatically when guide metadata matches the market/category.

Avoid creating multiple articles that answer essentially the same search intent. Expand a useful existing guide instead of producing doorway-like duplicates.

## Freshness

Time-sensitive guide details must be reviewed when events, construction, visitor-center information, government programs, incentives, seasonal market schedules or tourism projects change.

Evergreen topics should still be periodically checked for broken external links and stale internal directory paths.

## Suggested ongoing cadence

Prioritize quality over volume. A sustainable target is 2–4 substantive flagship guides per month plus updates to existing guides when source information changes. Use Search Intelligence, legitimate directory inventory and city/category SEO gaps to choose topics rather than publishing arbitrary city-keyword combinations.
